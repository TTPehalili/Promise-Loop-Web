# What PromiseLoop is

- PromiseLoop is an outsourcing company based in Manila, Philippines, providing collections as a service for service businesses in the USA.
- It works as the client's outsourced (offshore) collections team, not a traditional collection agency.
- Trained reps work as an extension of the client's office: under the client's company name, on the client's dialer, texting and email.
- Reps follow up on past-due accounts, get customers to commit to a payment date, and log every promise in a live pipeline.
- The client confirms when the money arrives, and the loop is closed.
- Reps always also name PromiseLoop on calls, for example: "This is Ana, calling for Lone Star HVAC with PromiseLoop."
- Positioning: turn unpaid jobs into a working pipeline, from write-off to paid, without burning the customer relationship.
- Brand promise: "Every promise, followed through."

## The loop

Every account runs the same five stages:

1. New: the account lands in the pipeline.
2. Contacting: a rep calls, by the rules, at a good time.
3. Promised: the customer commits to an amount and a date.
4. Waiting for you: the customer says they paid; the client checks.
5. Confirmed paid: the client confirms it. Loop closed.

"Loop" means closing the loop on each account. It never means calling repeatedly.

## How it works (three steps)

1. Plug us into your tools: PromiseLoop connects the client's dialer, texting, email and CRM, or the client uploads a CSV or invoice PDFs. Past-due accounts land in the pipeline as New.
2. Your offshore team calls: dedicated reps call, text and email customers in the client's company name, from the client's numbers, during legal hours, with scripts built around each job.
3. You confirm, we close the loop: customers pay the client directly. The client taps Confirmed paid, and the 5% applies only to what the client confirms.

## Dashboard

- Live pipeline: every account, every stage, every dollar.
- Promises due: what's coming in this week, day by day.
- Full activity log: every call, result and note, timestamped.
