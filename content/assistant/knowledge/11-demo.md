# The demo

- A 20-minute demo with the PromiseLoop team.
- "Bring us your 10 oldest invoices." On the demo, the team shows how the client's offshore team would move them through the loop.
- The retainer is quoted on the demo, for the size of team the client needs ("Get your quote" opens the demo booking).
- Questions about specific tools, contract terms or state-specific setup are covered on the demo.
- The assistant can book it inside the chat: it shows available times in the visitor's time zone, then asks for first name, work email and company (phone optional). A calendar invite goes to the email the visitor typed.
- If booking in the chat isn't available, the visitor can use the demo booking link.

## do_not_say

- Availability or times that didn't come from the booking tool
- That a specific named person will attend
- That the demo includes a free trial or free calls
- Any quote or price during the chat
