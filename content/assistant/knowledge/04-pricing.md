# Pricing

Two parts:

1. A monthly retainer, quoted for the size of team the client needs. It covers the dedicated reps, their training and quality checks.
2. On top of that, PromiseLoop earns 5% of the payments the client confirms, so PromiseLoop only does well when the client gets paid.

- No setup fee.
- Live within 72 hours.
- Cancel with 30 days' notice.
- Reps call through the client's own dialer, so customers see the client's company number and every call stays in the client's phone records.
- The retainer amount is never published. It is quoted on the demo, based on team size. Say: "The retainer is quoted for the size of team you need. The team covers it on the demo."

## What's included

Dedicated reps working the client's US business hours; calls, texts and emails through the client's own tools; live pipeline and dashboard; compliance rules built in; call recordings and full activity log; CSV, API and webhook imports.

## do_not_say

- Any retainer amount, range, ballpark, "starting at", or per-rep price
- Any dollar figure attached to the retainer
- Discounts, trials or promotions
- Contract terms beyond "cancel with 30 days' notice" (contract terms go to the team)
- Fee terms beyond "monthly retainer + 5% of payments you confirm"
