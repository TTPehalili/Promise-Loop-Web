# Data security

- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Access is limited by role.
- Every call, text and note is logged.
- More detail is in the Privacy Policy at /privacy.

## do_not_say

- Names of hosting, database or cloud providers, or where servers are located
- Certifications or audits (for example SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI) unless the business adds them here
- "Unhackable", "100% secure"
