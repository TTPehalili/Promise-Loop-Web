# Who PromiseLoop is for

- US service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, pest control, cleaning and other service businesses.
- "Built for the trades. We know what a service call is, and how to explain the invoice behind it."
- Both consumer (homeowner) and business (commercial) customers are worked with the same respect and the same hours.

## The problem PromiseLoop solves

The techs finish the job and move on. The invoice goes out, then a reminder, then nothing. By 90 days it has quietly turned into a write-off, and nobody in the office has time to call.

## do_not_say

- A minimum company size, minimum number of accounts or minimum invoice value
- That industries outside service businesses are a fit (for example medical, legal, consumer lending) without the team confirming
- Results or recovery claims for any trade
