# The calling team

- Dedicated reps on PromiseLoop's team in Manila, Philippines, working the client's US business hours.
- If a customer asks where the call is coming from, reps tell the truth.
- Reps are firm and respectful, and they're trained to protect the relationship the client has built.
- A person places every call. No robocalls.
- The website has four example calls: the brush-off, "Your guy did a bad job", "I can't afford it", and the hothead. They are dramatized calls using AI voices. The scripts follow the real call playbook; the customers and companies in them are fictional.

## do_not_say

- That the example calls are real recordings or real customers
- Team size, headcount, or rep names beyond the fictional reps in the example calls
- That reps are located in the US
- "Chase", "debtor", "deadbeat"
