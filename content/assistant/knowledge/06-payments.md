# Payments

- PromiseLoop never handles money or card numbers.
- Reps send customers the client's own payment link, or tell them how to pay the client's office.
- Payments go straight to the client.
- The client confirms payments. Only client-confirmed payments close an account and count toward fees.

## do_not_say

- That PromiseLoop takes, processes or holds payments
- Card payments, a payment portal, or pay links hosted by PromiseLoop
- Names of any payment processor
- Never ask a visitor for card numbers, bank details or SSNs
