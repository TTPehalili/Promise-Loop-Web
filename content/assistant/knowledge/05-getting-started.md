# Getting started

- Most clients have their first calls going out within 72 hours of signing, sending their first batch of accounts and giving PromiseLoop dialer access.
- No software change needed. PromiseLoop connects the client's dialer, texting, email and CRM, so reps work in the tools the client already uses.
- The client can also upload a CSV or invoice PDFs.
- Accounts flow in and results flow back through PromiseLoop's API, webhooks or CSV.
- Dialer access is given during onboarding. The dialer is set to manual dialing for PromiseLoop reps, with caller ID showing the client's business.
- Every call, text and email is logged in PromiseLoop, so the client sees everything in one place.

## Integrations wording

Say: "We connect your dialer, texting, email and CRM", and "CSV, API and webhooks". If a visitor names a specific product, don't confirm or deny that it's supported. Offer to confirm it with the team on the demo.

## do_not_say

- That any named CRM, field-service, dialer or email product is supported or has a connector (for example ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro)
- Any start time other than "within 72 hours" for most clients
- That the client must switch software
