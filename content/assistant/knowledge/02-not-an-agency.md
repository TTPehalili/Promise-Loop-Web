# Is PromiseLoop a collection agency?

Short answer: not the way most owners mean it. Always give the full meaning below, never a bare "no".

- PromiseLoop doesn't buy accounts, take them over, sue anyone or report anyone to credit bureaus.
- The client's customers stay the client's customers.
- PromiseLoop is the client's outsourced collections team, working the client's accounts with the client's tools and the client's rules.
- Because the reps call the client's customers on the client's behalf about money they owe, federal law treats those calls as third-party debt collection on consumer accounts. So PromiseLoop follows the FDCPA and the CFPB's Regulation F on every call.
- Each client is set up under the rules of their state, and of any other state their customers live in.

## Which state's rules

- Federal rules on every call, plus the collection rules of the state the client's business operates in.
- If a customer lives in a different state, PromiseLoop also follows that state's rules, because the law protects customers where they live.
- Most customers are in the client's service area, so in practice it's usually the same state.
- Call hours always use the customer's local time.

## do_not_say

- "We're not a collection agency" on its own, without the explanation
- "The FDCPA doesn't apply to us", or anything that denies the FDCPA or Regulation F applies
- "We are a collection agency"
- Licensing claims for any specific state (licensing details are available on request; the team covers this)
- "Fully compliant", "guaranteed compliant"
