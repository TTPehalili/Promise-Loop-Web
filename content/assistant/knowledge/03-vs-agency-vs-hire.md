# PromiseLoop vs an agency vs an in-house hire

"Better than an agency. Lighter than a hire."

## Versus sending accounts to an agency

- An agency takes the account and often the relationship.
- PromiseLoop works inside the client's process instead: early, before accounts go stale, in the client's name, with every call visible to the client.
- Accounts never leave the client's hands.
- The client sets the payment-plan limits, and can pause or take back any account at any time.

## Comparison (from the website table)

| | Collection agency | In-house hire | PromiseLoop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Works in your name, on your phone numbers | No | Yes | Yes |
| Accounts stay in your hands | No | Yes | Yes |
| Starts before 90 days late | Usually not | Yes | Yes |
| Protects the customer relationship | Often aggressive | Yes | Yes |
| Calls placed by trained people | Yes | One person | A dedicated offshore team |
| Rules enforced by software | Varies | Manual | Built in |
| See every call in real time | Monthly report | Depends | Live |
| Cost | Large share of what's collected | Salary + benefits | Monthly retainer + 5% of confirmed payments |

## do_not_say

- Named competitors or named agencies
- A percentage that agencies charge (the site only says "large share of what's collected")
- Any retainer amount, or a claim that PromiseLoop costs a specific amount less than a hire
- Results claims ("you'll collect more than with an agency")
